### Screen Recording Guide

#### Section A: Extension UI Demo (0:25–0:45)

**Equipment needed**: Screen recording software (OBS/ScreenFlow/CleanShot), Chrome with extension installed.

**Steps**:
1. Open Chrome, navigate to LinkedIn Sales Navigator
2. Show empty page, then click the AutoLead extension icon in the toolbar
3. Extension panel slides out: show header "AutoLead Messenger v2.0"
4. Highlight the "Capture" tab (default view)
5. Select text on the LinkedIn page (simulate auto-detect by showing a sample lead card)
6. Show extracted fields: Name, Company, Title, Email, Phone (if available)
7. Switch to "Templates" tab
8. Show template gallery: 5 pre-built templates visible
9. Click "New Template" — show AI variable inserter
10. Highlight merge tags: {{firstName}}, {{lastName}}, {{company}}, {{title}}, {{date}}
11. Show "AI Rewrite" button (simulates personalization)
12. Switch to "Send" tab
13. Show connected services: "Google Voice (Connected)", "WhatsApp (Configure)", "Email (Configure)"
14. Select Google Voice
15. Show "Send Test" button — click it
16. Show confirmation toast: "Message sent to John Smith via Google Voice"
17. Switch to "Analytics" tab — show mock dashboard with charts

**Camera angle**: Top-down screen capture. Add cursor highlight effect.
**Audio**: Light "click" sound effects for each interaction. Subtle hover sound.

#### Section B: Lead-Sending Workflow (0:45–1:30)

**Steps**:
1. Start from a fresh LinkedIn search results page
2. Click the AutoLead icon → "Bulk Send" mode
3. Show selection checkboxes appearing on each lead card
4. Select 5 leads (click checkboxes)
5. Show panel updating: "5 leads selected"
6. Click "Send Messages"
7. Modal appears: "Review & Send"
   - Show message preview for each lead (personalized)
   - Show channel selector (default: Google Voice)
   - Show rate limit: "5 messages every 60 seconds"
8. Click "Confirm Send"
9. Progress bar: "Sending 5 messages..."
10. Each message shows status: "Sent" or "Delivered" (green checkmarks)
11. Completion: "All 5 messages sent successfully"
12. Show "View in Analytics" button — click it
13. Analytics dashboard: open rate 80%, response rate 30%

**Camera angle**: Screen capture with picture-in-picture (optional) of presenter's hands on keyboard.
**Audio**: Subtle progress sound (ascending tone as messages send). Notification "ding" for completions.

### Hook Variations (A/B Test Options)

**Hook A (Time-based)**: "3 hours wasted on 50 messages? Sarah reclaimed her day with AutoLead."
- Strength: Specificity, relatable time waste
- Best for: LinkedIn ads, direct outreach

**Hook B (Result-based)**: "50 messages → 200 messages. 3x response rate. All on autopilot."
- Strength: Clear transformation, numbers
- Best for: Product page hero, landing pages

**Hook C (Question-based)**: "What would you do with 15 extra hours per week?"
- Strength: Interactive, curiosity
- Best for: Email campaigns, top-of-funnel
